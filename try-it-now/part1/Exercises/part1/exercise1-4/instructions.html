How to Follow a Recipe Correctly

These instructions are for anyone who wants to prepare a meal safely, especially someone who is busy, tired, or in a hurry. Follow each step carefully to avoid mistakes and prepare the food correctly.

What You Need

  • A recipe with clear, step-by-step instructions.
  • All the ingredients listed in the recipe.
  • Measuring cups and spoons.
  • A clean bowl, cooking pot, and other necessary utensils.
  • A clean kitchen and a safe cooking area.
  • A timer or clock to check the cooking time.

Steps

  1. Read the entire recipe before you begin cooking.
  2. Check that you have all the required ingredients and equipment.
  3. Wash your hands and clean your cooking area.
  4. Measure each ingredient according to the recipe.
  5. Follow the instructions in the correct order without skipping any steps.
  6. Set the cooking temperature and time as stated in the recipe.
  7. Never leave cooking food unattended if it could burn or cause a fire.
  8. Check that the food is properly cooked before serving it.
  9. Turn off the stove or other cooking equipment when you finish.
  10. Clean your equipment and store any leftover food safely.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you make a mistake, stop and check the recipe before continuing. If the food smells burnt, turn off the heat and check it carefully. If you are unsure whether food is safe to eat, do not eat it. Always follow the recipe carefully, because a small mistake can affect the taste, quality, and safety of your food.
