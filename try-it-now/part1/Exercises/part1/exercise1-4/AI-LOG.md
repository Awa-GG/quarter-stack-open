cold recall question
Write down the difference between <strong> and
<b> , and between <em> and <i> . Then check yourself against 

1. Difference between <strong> and <b>
<strong>: Shows that text is important. It gives the text strong importance or seriousness.
<b>: Makes text visually bold without indicating that it is more important.

2. Difference between <em> and <i>
<em>: Emphasizes text. The meaning of the sentence may change depending on which word is emphasized.
<i>: Displays text in italics without indicating that it needs special emphasis. It can be used for technical terms or foreign words.
