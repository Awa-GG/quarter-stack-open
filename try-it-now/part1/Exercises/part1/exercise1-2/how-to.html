How to Apply for a Gambian National Identity Card

This guide explains how to prepare for and complete the Gambian National Identity Management System Citizen Application Form. It covers the information applicants need to provide and the final declaration and authentication sections.

Before You Go

Before completing the application, decide whether you are applying for a new identity card, replacing a lost or damaged card, requesting a reissue, or updating your information. Prepare the personal details you will need to enter on the form.

What to Bring

The application form lists several types of documents that can be used for verification. Prepare the relevant document and check that the information you provide matches your supporting records.

  • Birth certificate
  • Seyfo's certificate
  • Alkalo's attestation
  • Passport
  • Voter's ID
  • Naturalization certificate, where applicable
  • Other listed verification documents, where applicable

At the Office

Complete the application form carefully. Provide your personal details, birth information, residential address, occupation, and the other information requested in the relevant sections.

Provide Your Personal Information

Enter your surname, forenames, date of birth, place of birth, nationality, hometown, occupation, and residential address. Complete the parentage section with the requested information about your father and mother.

Provide Verification and Contact Details

Identify the verification document you are providing and enter the requested document details. Complete the contact section with your available phone numbers and email address.

After Completing the Form

Read the declaration carefully before signing or making your thumbprint. The declaration confirms that the information and documents provided are genuine and correct. The form also contains sections for the interviewer and enrolment officer.

Authentication

The final section is for authentication. The form asks the authenticator to certify that the applicant is personally known to them and that the information provided is believed to be true and correct. It also requests the authenticator's National Identification Number, signature, and date.
