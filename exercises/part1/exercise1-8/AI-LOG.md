I asked ChatGPT to check whether my HTML page was now valid after correcting the errors. I received confirmation that the validator showed no errors or warnings.

ChatGPT helped me to:

* Understand HTML validator error messages.

* Understand the correct nesting of HTML elements.
* Add HTML comments explaining my corrections.
* Understand the difference between `errors-found.txt` and `AI-LOG.md`.

5. What I learned

I learned how to use the W3C HTML Validator to identify problems and check my corrections.

Most importantly, I learned that I should fix one error at a time, save my work, and validate the page again.
