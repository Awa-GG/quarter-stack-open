1. answer:
<ol> (Ordered List): Use it when the items must follow a specific order, such as steps in a process.
<ul> (Unordered List): Use it when the items do not need to follow a specific order, such as a list of things to bring.

2. answer:2. Where does a nested list go?

A nested list is a list inside another list. It must be placed inside the <li> element of the parent list, after the parent item's text and before the closing </li> tag.
